I am a Senior Staff Researcher and team lead at [Criteo AI Lab](https://ailab.criteo.com/) in Paris, where I lead the **Commerce Foundation Models** team. Before joining Criteo in 2017, I was an Associate Professor at Université de Lille and a member of the Inria SequeL team, where I defended my HDR in 2015. I received my PhD from Université Paris XI (Orsay) in 2005.

Trying to do science within the LLM craziness.

## Current Research

My team works on the long-term research frontier of foundation models for commerce:

* **Deep user modeling and generative recommendation**: sequential models of user timelines at the scale of millions of products, and *semantic IDs*, which turn product embeddings into discrete tokens that generative recommenders can use.
* **LLMs for recommendation**: adapting, distilling and studying reasoning in large language models on recommendation data.
* **Generative 3D and world models**: navigable immersive 3D scenes, 3D-aware latent spaces and world models for immersive content.
* **Catalog structuring with foundation models**: using LLMs to build and enrich product taxonomies at scale.
* **LLM-assisted research**: testing how LLMs can take part in writing mathematical papers.

Over the years I have also worked on reinforcement learning and bandits, active learning, fair machine learning, and vision and language.
