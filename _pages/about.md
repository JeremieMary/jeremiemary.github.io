## Current Research

My team works on the long-term research frontier of foundation models for commerce:

<!-- One card per research area: a bold title line, a blank line, then one sentence. -->
<div class="home-research" markdown="1">

<div class="home-card" markdown="1">
**Deep user modeling and generative recommendation**

Sequential models of user timelines at the scale of millions of products, and *semantic IDs*, which turn product embeddings into discrete tokens that generative recommenders can use.

</div>

<div class="home-card" markdown="1">
**LLMs for recommendation**

Adapting, distilling and studying reasoning in large language models on recommendation data.

</div>

<div class="home-card" markdown="1">
**Generative 3D and world models**

Navigable immersive 3D scenes, 3D-aware latent spaces and world models for immersive content.

</div>

<div class="home-card" markdown="1">
**Catalog structuring with foundation models**

Using LLMs to build and enrich product taxonomies at scale.

</div>

<div class="home-card" markdown="1">
**LLM-assisted research**

Testing how LLMs can take part in writing mathematical papers.

</div>

</div>

Over the years I have also worked on reinforcement learning and bandits, active learning, fair machine learning, and vision and language.
